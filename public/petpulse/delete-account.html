Delete your PetPulse account

You can delete your account and all the data we store for it at any time. Here's how.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open PetPulse and tap More.
  2. Tap your name at the top to open your account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm. You may be asked to sign in again first.

Deletion happens straight away.

Option 2: ask us by email

If you no longer have the app, email us from the address you signed in with. Put "Delete my PetPulse account" in the subject. We'll confirm it's you and delete your account within 30 days, then let you know when it's done.

Request deletion by email

If you signed in with Apple using "Hide my email", tell us the relay address, which you can find in your iPhone under Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple → PetPulse.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: name, email address and sign-in.
  • Pet records you backed up: pets, photos, vaccines, weigh-ins, care, medicines, doses, diary entries and contacts.
  • Playdates you are hosting.
  • Your place in any shared household. If others still share the household, they keep the shared pets' records; if you were the only member, the whole household is deleted.

What we keep

  • Purchase records, held by Apple, Google and our subscription provider for as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Backup copies in our cloud provider, which expire within about 30 days.
  • Support emails, kept for up to 2 years unless you ask us to delete them too.
Deleting your account doesn't cancel Premium. Cancel your subscription in the App Store or Google Play first, as explained on our support page. Records saved only on your phone are removed when you delete the app.

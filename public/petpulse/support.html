Common questions

I'm not getting reminders

Check that notifications are allowed for PetPulse in your phone's Settings, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't hiding them. In the app, open More → Reminders to check the reminder time. On Android, also allow "Alarms & reminders" and turn off battery optimisation for PetPulse.

My records aren't showing on my new phone

Sign in with the same account you used before (Apple, Google or email). Records only sync if you were signed in on the old phone. Open More and tap your name to see when the last sync happened.

How do I share my pets with family?

Family sharing is part of Premium. Go to More, tap your name, then Share with family, and send the invite code. It works for 7 days, and up to 6 people can share the same pets.

The card scan read something wrong

Scanning works best on a flat card in good light. Always check the details before saving; you can edit any vaccine afterwards.

How do I change the app language?

Go to More → Language. English is free; Español, Français, Deutsch, Português and हिन्दी are part of Premium.

A pet has passed away or been rehomed

We're sorry. Open the pet's profile and tap "[name] is no longer with me", then choose what happened. Every reminder for them stops, and their records and photos stay safe.

Billing

  • Cancel on iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → PetPulse → Cancel Subscription.
  • Cancel on Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → PetPulse → Cancel.
  • Refunds: request them from Apple or Google Play. We can't issue refunds ourselves.
  • Restore Premium after reinstalling: More → PetPulse Premium → Restore purchase, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account.

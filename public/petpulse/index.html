Questions

Good to know

Is PetPulse free?

Yes. The free plan covers one pet with vaccines, weight, care records and reminders. Premium adds unlimited pets, family sharing, PDF reports and more, as a monthly or yearly subscription you can cancel at any time.

Do I need an account?

No. PetPulse works on your phone without signing up. Sign in with Apple, Google or email when you want your records backed up, synced to a new phone, or shared with family.

Which pets does it support?

Dogs, cats, rabbits, birds, parrots, fish, hamsters, guinea pigs, turtles, horses and more. Each species gets care that makes sense for it.

Can my family see the same pets?

With Premium, up to 6 people can share the same pets. When someone logs a dose or a walk, everyone sees it straight away.

Does PetPulse replace my vet?

No. PetPulse helps you keep records and remember care, but it doesn't give medical advice. Always ask your vet about your pet's health, and contact an emergency vet if you are worried.

What happens if a pet passes away or is rehomed?

You can mark a pet as remembered, rehomed or missing. PetPulse quietly stops every reminder for them and keeps their records and photos safe, free on every plan.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to More, tap your name, then Delete account. You can also request deletion here.

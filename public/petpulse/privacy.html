2. What we collect

When you create an account

Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Apple, Google or email, we receive your name, email address and a user ID from our sign-in provider (Firebase Authentication). If you use "Hide my email" with Apple, we only see the relay address.

Pet records you back up

When you are signed in, these records are stored in our cloud database so they sync across your devices and with family members you invite:

  • Pet profiles (name, species, breed, sex, birthday, notes, and a small copy of the profile photo)
  • Vaccinations, weigh-ins and care records (treatments, vet visits, grooming and similar)
  • Medicines, doses and symptom-diary entries
  • Contacts you type in, such as your vet or pet sitter

Pet health records are about your animals, not you, but notes you write may contain personal details, so we protect them the same way.

Family sharing

If you join or create a shared household, the other members can see your name, email address and the shared pets' records. Invite codes expire after 7 days.

Playdates (Premium)

If you host a playdate, the title, time, place name, the place's map coordinates, your first name, your pet's name and species, and any notes are visible to other signed-in PetPulse users nearby. When you join a playdate, your name and your pet's name are shown to other attendees. Please only choose public places.

Purchases

Subscriptions are paid through Apple or Google; we never see your card details. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, receives your purchase receipts, your PetPulse user ID and basic device information so it can confirm your Premium access on every device.

Support emails

If you email us, we keep the message and your email address so we can reply.

3. What stays on your phone

These are stored only on your device and are never sent to us:

  • Everything you log while not signed in
  • Photos in a pet's photo gallery
  • Service Dog Hub records (your working dog's profile, alert log, training and application tracker)
  • Photos of vaccine cards. Text is read by your phone's built-in text recognition
  • Reminders, which are scheduled as local notifications on your phone
  • Calendar events you choose to add to your phone's calendar

If you delete the app without signing in, this information is deleted with it.

6. Ads and tracking

The free plan shows a small number of ads from Google AdMob. Premium never shows ads.

  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), we ask for your consent first using Google's consent form. You can change your choice any time in More → Ad privacy choices.
  • On iPhone, ads are only personalised if you allow tracking when iOS asks. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings.

Google may collect device identifiers, IP address and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

7. Location

PetPulse only asks for your location while you use features that need it: the vet finder, pet-friendly places, playdates and the Service Dog Hub's "your rights where you live". We use it to search nearby and to work out your country and state, using your phone's built-in map service. We don't track your location in the background or store your location history. If you host a playdate, the place's coordinates are shared as described above.

8. How long we keep it

  • Account and cloud records: until you delete them or your account. Deleted records are removed from our database straight away. Backup copies kept by our cloud provider expire within about 30 days.
  • Playdates: until the host cancels, or when the host's account is deleted.
  • Purchase records: as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Support emails: up to 2 years after the conversation ends.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU GDPR, the UK GDPR or US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA, you may have the right to access, correct, download, or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict some uses, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

  • Correct: edit any record or your name in the app.
  • Download: share a vet report from any pet's profile, or email us for a full copy.
  • Delete: More → your name → Delete account, or request deletion here.
  • Anything else: email askdevelopers001@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days and won't treat you differently for using your rights.

Grievances (India): if you are unhappy with how we handle your personal data, email askdevelopers001@gmail.com with the subject "Grievance". We will respond within 30 days. If you are still not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our cloud provider. Database rules make sure only members of your household can read or change your pets' records. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password if you sign in with email.

11. Children

PetPulse is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. Accounts are meant for adults; younger family members can use PetPulse on a parent's device. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. International transfers

Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where we transfer personal information out of the EEA, UK or Switzerland, we rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses and our providers' data processing terms.

13. This website

This website has no ads, analytics or tracking cookies. It remembers only your light or dark mode choice, in your own browser. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts load from Google Fonts, so GitHub and Google receive your IP address and browser details when you visit, under their own privacy policies.

14. Changes and contact

If we make important changes, we will tell you in the app or by email before they take effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Questions

Good to know.

Which technologies do you work with?

Web, iOS, Android and cross-platform apps, AI and data, Azure, AWS and Google Cloud, DevOps and security. The full toolkit is on the Services page.

How do we start?

Send a short email. We set up a call, then send a written scope and estimate.

Can you take over an existing app or website?

Yes. We start with a short review of the code and hosting, then propose fixes and next steps.

What does a project cost?

It depends on the scope. After the first call you get a written estimate, with no obligation.

Where are you based?

Our headquarters is in North York, Ontario, Canada, where Jay meets clients. Design and engineering are done with our studio partner, Ask Developers, in India.

Have a product in mind?

Tell us what you want to build. You get a clear plan, a written estimate and one team from the first idea to launch and beyond.

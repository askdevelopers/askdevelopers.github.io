Services

Pick a layer, or hand us all five.

Most projects touch more than one layer. One team means no hand-offs between agencies.

  1. 01ExperienceWhat people see and touch.Web apps · iOS · Android +5
    • Web appsSaaS products, dashboards, portals and marketplaces on React, Next.js, Node.js or Laravel.
    • iOSNative Swift and SwiftUI apps, from the first build to the App Store release.
    • AndroidNative Kotlin apps, built, tested and released on Google Play.
    • React Native & FlutterOne codebase for iOS and Android that still feels native on both.
    • UX / UI designResearch, wireframes, Figma prototypes and design systems.
    • Accessibility (WCAG & AODA)Audits and fixes so your website and apps meet WCAG AA and Ontario’s AODA requirements.
    • Motion & animationCalm, purposeful animation for apps, websites and product demos.
    • Brand & graphicsLogos, icons, store screenshots and marketing graphics.
  2. 02IntelligenceData and AI that do real work.AI integration · AI agents & assistants · AI governance & safety +3
    • AI integrationGPT, Claude, Azure OpenAI or open models wired into your product, not bolted on.
    • AI agents & assistantsAssistants that call your tools and APIs, with a person in the loop where it matters.
    • AI governance & safetyClear rules for data, review and access, so AI features are safe to ship and easy to audit.
    • Machine learningFine-tuning, forecasting, classification and recommendations, measured with evaluation sets.
    • Data engineeringPipelines and warehouses that give your teams and models clean data they can trust.
    • Analytics & BIDashboards and reports that answer the questions you actually ask.
  3. 03PlatformWhere it runs, and how it ships.Cloud migration · Cloud hosting · Legacy modernisation +6
    • Cloud migrationMove from on-premise servers or another cloud with a tested rollback plan.
    • Cloud hostingWebsites and apps hosted, updated, backed up and watched on Azure, AWS or Google Cloud.
    • Legacy modernisationRebuild ageing .NET, PHP or desktop systems piece by piece while the old one keeps running.
    • Canadian data residencyApps and data hosted in Canadian regions of Azure, AWS or Google Cloud when your clients require it.
    • Microsoft AzureApp Service, Functions, AKS, Azure OpenAI and AI Search, set up in your own tenant.
    • AWSLambda, ECS, RDS and S3, described as code so every environment matches.
    • Google CloudCloud Run, Firebase and BigQuery for lean, fast-moving products.
    • DevOps & CI/CDAutomated builds, tests and releases for web, iOS and Android.
    • Containers & KubernetesDocker images and Kubernetes clusters sized for your real traffic.
  4. 04ShieldSecurity around every layer.Security assessments · Penetration testing · Vulnerability management +11
    • Security assessmentsA clear review of the risks in your apps, cloud and code, with fixes in priority order.
    • Penetration testingTesting your web and mobile apps the way an attacker would, before one does.
    • Vulnerability managementRegular scans of your apps, servers and libraries, with every finding tracked until it is fixed.
    • Secure code reviewYour code read for injection, login and data-leak flaws, starting with the OWASP Top 10.
    • Cloud securityAzure, AWS and Google Cloud reviewed and hardened: access, networks, keys, logging and backups.
    • Website security & hardeningHTTPS, security headers, firewall rules, malware clean-up and safe updates for websites and CMS.
    • Identity & accessSign-in, roles and permissions, single sign-on and multi-factor authentication.
    • Email & domain securitySPF, DKIM and DMARC set up properly, so no one can send email in your company’s name.
    • DevSecOpsSecurity checks in every build: dependency and secret scanning, and safe defaults.
    • Security monitoring & incident responseAlerts on suspicious activity, and a clear plan for who does what when something goes wrong.
    • Backup & disaster recoveryTested backups and recovery plans, so ransomware or an outage is a bad day, not the end.
    • Security policies & roadmapPolicies, a risk register and a 12-month security plan sized for your business.
    • Security awareness trainingShort, practical sessions and phishing drills that help your team spot attacks.
    • PIPEDA, Law 25 & SOC 2 readinessGetting ready for Canadian privacy law and SOC 2 audits, step by step.
  5. 05CareKeeping it healthy after launch.QA & test automation · Maintenance & support · Managed services +2
    • QA & test automationManual and automated testing across real devices and browsers.
    • Maintenance & supportUpdates, monitoring, OS upgrades and fixes long after launch.
    • Managed servicesMonitoring, patching, backups and cost reviews for your apps and cloud, on a monthly plan.
    • IT consultingArchitecture reviews, technology choices and honest second opinions.
    • Team augmentationOur engineers join your team, in your tools and your sprints.

How we work

Six steps, no surprises.

You always know what is being built, what is next and what it costs.

  1. 01

    Discover

    We learn the problem, the users and the constraints, then agree scope and price in writing.

  2. 02

    Design

    Flows, screens and architecture you can click through before a line of production code.

  3. 03

    Build

    Short cycles with a working build every week. You see progress, not status reports.

  4. 04

    Test

    Automated and manual testing on real devices for reliability and security.

  5. 05

    Launch

    Release to the stores or the web with a rollback plan and full handover documents.

  6. 06

    Support

    Maintenance, updates and new features. We stay your technology partner after launch.

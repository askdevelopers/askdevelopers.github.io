Super Fit Women · Policies

Privacy policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026

The short version
  • You don't need an account. Your profile, workouts, weight, food, water and step history are stored only on your phone.
  • We don't sell your personal data, and we never use your health or body information for advertising.
  • The Basic version shows ads. The app also uses Google services for analytics, crash reports, notifications and purchases. These services may collect device identifiers, as explained below.
  • Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes everything stored on your phone. How to delete your data

1. Who we are

The Super Fit Women app ("the app") is developed and published by Ask Developers ("we", "us"), a software development agency. Ask Developers is responsible for the app, for this website and for this policy.

Contact: askdevelopers001@gmail.com · Ask Developers website

2. Information stored on your phone

To build your plan and show your progress, the app saves the following locally on your device. This information is not uploaded to our servers.

  • Profile details you enter, such as goal, gender, age, height, weight and target weight
  • Workout history, completed days, streaks, badges, habits, custom workouts and how a workout felt
  • Water intake, food log, fasting sessions, daily steps and BMI
  • Backup files you create, saved only where you choose (for example your Google Drive)
  • Settings such as reminder times, voice coach, units, language and theme

We can't see, access or recover this information. If you share a badge or a report, it goes only to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp or Instagram), and that app's privacy policy then applies.

3. Health Connect and step data

Steps are optional. If you turn them on, the app asks for these permissions:

  • Read steps from Health Connect, and physical activity to count steps with your phone's step sensor.
  • Write exercise, active calories burned and weight to Health Connect, so other health apps you use can see your workouts and weigh-ins.

Data read from Health Connect or the step sensor is used only to show your steps, distance and calorie estimates inside the app. It stays on your phone. We don't send it to our servers, share it with third parties, sell it, or use it for advertising, and no person at Ask Developers can read it. You can disconnect at any time in the app's Health tab or in Health Connect.

5. Your consent choices

If you live in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before ads are personalized and before analytics data is collected. You can change your answer at any time in Me → Privacy choices.

6. Permissions the app asks for

  • Internet and network state: to load ads, demo videos, notifications and purchases. Workouts still work offline.
  • Notifications: for workout, water and fasting reminders. You can turn them off at any time.
  • Alarms and start on boot: so reminders fire at the time you chose, even after the phone restarts.
  • Physical activity and Health Connect (only if you turn steps on): see section 3.
  • Google Play billing: to buy Premium.
  • No camera, photo, contact or location access: the app doesn't request these permissions.

7. How we use information

We use information only to run the app, build your plan, send the reminders you set, show ads in the Basic version, process and verify purchases, and understand overall usage so that we can fix problems and improve the app. We don't sell your personal data.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

Data on your phone stays until you delete it. To remove everything, clear the app's data in Android Settings → Apps → Super Fit Women → Storage, or uninstall the app. Reset plan in the app clears your workout progress. Third-party services keep data according to their own policies. Step-by-step instructions are on the Delete your data page.

9. Children

The app isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we'll help remove it.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or the EU GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to withdraw consent. Because the app stores your personal data only on your device, you can do most of this directly in the app. For anything else, or to raise a grievance, email askdevelopers001@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 30 days.

11. Security

Your data on the device is protected by Android's app sandbox. Connections to third-party services use HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

12. This website

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or ads. It only remembers your light or dark theme choice in your own browser. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

Tester sign-up. If you join our tester program on the home page, we collect the email address you enter and, only if you add them, your name and phone number. They are stored in Google Cloud Firestore (part of Firebase) and used only to add you to the app's test on Google Play, send you the invite link and contact you about the test. We don't share them with anyone else or use them for marketing. We delete them within 90 days after the test ends, or sooner if you ask: email askdevelopers001@gmail.com. When Google Play adds you as a tester, Google's own privacy policy applies to your Play account.

13. Health disclaimer

The app provides general fitness guidance and is not medical advice. Read the full health and safety disclaimer.

14. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we'll post the new version on this page and change the effective date above. For significant changes, we'll also let you know in the app.

Super Fit Women · Policies

Delete your data

Effective date: 29 October 2026

The short version
  • The app has no account, so there is no account to delete.
  • Your data is stored only on your phone. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes it.
  • Deleting data doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play.

1. Delete everything on your phone

Open Android Settings → Apps → Super Fit Women → Storage and tap Clear storage (or Clear data). Uninstalling the app does the same. This permanently deletes your profile, workout history, streaks, badges, weight, water, food, fasting and step history, and your settings. It can't be undone.

2. Delete only your workout progress

In the app, use Reset plan to start your plan again from day 1. Your other data stays.

3. Delete your backups

If you used Backup & restore, the backup file is stored where you saved it, for example in your Google Drive or your phone's files. Delete that file there. We don't keep a copy.

4. Delete data shared with Health Connect

If you connected the app to Health Connect, workouts, active calories and weight the app wrote stay in Health Connect after you uninstall. To delete them, open Health Connect → App permissions → Super Fit Women, turn off the permissions and choose Delete app data. The exact steps may differ by device.

5. Ads and analytics data

Ad networks and Google Analytics for Firebase use a device or app instance identifier, not your name. To break the link to past data, go to Settings → Google → Ads and reset or delete your advertising ID. Analytics data is kept for a limited time under Google's retention settings and then deleted. If you live in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you can also withdraw consent in the app at Me → Privacy choices.

To ask us for help with data held by these services, email askdevelopers001@gmail.com. We aim to reply within 30 days.

6. Subscriptions and purchase records

Deleting app data doesn't cancel Premium. To stop future charges, cancel in Google Play as explained in Subscriptions & refunds. Google keeps its own record of your purchases under the Google privacy policy.

Health & safety disclaimer

Effective date: 29 October 2026

Please read before you start
  • The app gives general fitness guidance. It is not medical advice and doesn't replace your doctor.
  • Check with your doctor before starting, especially if you are pregnant, have recently given birth, or have a health condition or injury.
  • Stop right away if anything hurts or feels wrong.

1. Not medical advice

The workouts, plans, programs, food log, meal ideas, fasting plans, step goals and tips in the app are for general information and fitness only. They are not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease, and they don't replace advice from a doctor, midwife, physiotherapist or registered dietitian.

2. Check with your doctor first

Talk to your doctor before you start any new exercise, diet or fasting plan, and especially if you:

  • are pregnant, trying to get pregnant, have recently given birth or are breastfeeding
  • have a heart, lung, blood pressure, joint, bone or back condition
  • have diabetes or another long-term condition, or take regular medicine
  • have an injury, or haven't exercised for a long time
  • have, or have had, an eating disorder

3. Pregnancy and postpartum programs

Follow your doctor's or midwife's advice over anything in the app. Start a postpartum program only after you have been cleared for exercise, usually around 6 weeks after birth and often later after a C-section. Stop right away and contact your doctor or midwife if you notice:

  • pain in your belly, pelvis, chest or back
  • dizziness, feeling faint or short of breath
  • vaginal bleeding or leaking fluid
  • headache, blurred vision or swelling
  • contractions or a heavy, pressing feeling

4. Listen to your body

Go at a pace that feels right, warm up, keep breathing, drink water and skip any move that feels wrong. Stop exercising and get medical help if you feel chest pain, severe shortness of breath, dizziness or sharp pain. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

5. Food and fasting

Calorie targets, meal ideas and fasting plans are general suggestions. Fasting isn't suitable for everyone, for example during pregnancy or breastfeeding, for people under 18, or for people with diabetes or a history of eating disorders. Ask a doctor or dietitian before changing how you eat.

6. Estimates, not measurements

Calories burned, BMI, body measurements, steps, distance and targets shown in the app are estimates based on what you enter and on general formulas. They may not be accurate for you and shouldn't be used for medical decisions.

7. Results vary

Everyone's body is different. Results depend on many things, such as your starting point, health, sleep, food and how often you train. We can't promise a specific amount of weight loss or any other result.

8. Your responsibility

You choose which workouts and plans to follow, and you do them at your own risk. Make sure you have enough space, a non-slip surface and stable furniture for any chair or wall exercises. To the extent the law allows, Ask Developers isn't responsible for any injury or health problem resulting from the use of the app. See our terms of use.

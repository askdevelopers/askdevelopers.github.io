Subscriptions & refunds

Effective date: 29 October 2026

The short version
  • Premium is sold through Google Play as a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel. Cancel in Google Play at any time.
  • If you cancel during a trial, you won't be charged.
  • Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy. Email us if something went wrong and we'll help.

1. Premium options

  • Monthly: billed every month.
  • Yearly: billed once a year. New customers may get a trial first, shown on the purchase screen.
  • Lifetime: a single payment that unlocks Premium for as long as the app is offered.

The price, currency and any trial are shown in the app and in the Google Play purchase window before you confirm. Prices can differ between countries and may include local taxes.

2. Payment

All payments are processed by Google Play and charged to the payment method in your Google account. We never see your card or payment details.

3. Trials

If a trial is offered, Premium starts right away. Your subscription begins and you are charged when the trial ends, unless you cancel before then. If you cancel during the trial, you won't be charged. Each Google account can use a trial once.

4. Automatic renewal

Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period, at the then-current price, until you cancel. If the price changes, Google Play will let you know in advance and, where required, ask you to accept the new price.

5. How to cancel

  • Open the Google Play Store app, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Choose Super Fit Women and tap Cancel subscription.
  • Or, in the app, go to Me → Premium active → Manage.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data does not cancel a subscription. After you cancel, Premium stays active until the end of the period you've paid for. The app then returns to Basic, and your progress stays on your phone.

6. Refunds

Because purchases are made through Google Play, Google Play's refund policy applies.

  • Within 48 hours of a purchase, you can usually request a refund directly from Google Play. See Google Play refund help.
  • After 48 hours, email us at askdevelopers001@gmail.com with your Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.). We'll review your request and help where we can.
  • We don't give partial refunds for the unused part of a subscription period, except where the law requires it.

Nothing here limits any refund rights you have under the consumer laws of your country.

7. Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, Google Play retries for a short time and lets you update your payment method. Premium may pause until the payment goes through.

8. New phone or reinstall

Premium is linked to the Google account you bought it with. Sign in to Google Play with the same account, open the app and Premium is restored automatically.

9. Premium access codes

Premium access codes are given to specific people and aren't purchased, so they have no refund value.

10. Contact us

Billing questions: askdevelopers001@gmail.com

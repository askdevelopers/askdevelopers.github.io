In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Brainik and go to the Me tab.
  2. Tap the Settings (gear) button.
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account, then confirm.

If you signed in with Apple, you'll be asked to confirm with Apple once more, and Brainik's access to your Apple ID is revoked.

Without the app

Email askdevelopers001@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, or include your Player ID (shown at the bottom of Settings). Use the subject "Delete my Brainik account". We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account (name, email, account ID)
  • Your cloud save: levels, stars, scores, streaks, coins, gems, trophies and settings
  • Your notification device registrations

What is kept

  • Purchase records needed for tax, accounting and fraud prevention, kept for up to 7 years and not linked to an active account
  • Analytics and crash data, which are not linked to your name or email and expire automatically within 14 months

Subscriptions are not cancelled by deleting your account. Cancel Brainik Pro in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

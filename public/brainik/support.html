Contact us

Email askdevelopers001@gmail.com. We aim to reply within a few days.

To help us find the problem quickly, please include:

  • your Player ID, if you’re signed in (in the app: Me → Settings, at the bottom; tap it to copy)
  • your phone model and its iOS or Android version
  • what happened, and a screenshot if you can

You can also open the email from inside the app: Me → Settings → Support → Contact support. It fills in your Player ID, app version and phone model for you.

Purchases and Brainik Pro

Restore purchases on a new or reset phone

Sign in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with, open Brainik and tap Restore purchases in the Shop or in Settings. Brainik Pro and Remove Ads come back at no cost. Gem packs are one-time purchases, so the store can’t restore them; if you were signed in, your gems and coins come back with your account.

Cancel or change Brainik Pro

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google, not inside Brainik, and they renew automatically until you cancel:

  • iPhone and iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Brainik. Or open apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.
  • Android: Google Play → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Brainik. Or open play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date (or before a free trial ends) to avoid being charged. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app or your Brainik account does not cancel a subscription.

Refunds

Payments are taken by Apple or Google, so refunds are handled by them under their policies:

  • Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Google Play: Google Play refund requests

If something you bought didn’t arrive, email us with your Player ID and the store receipt, and we’ll sort it out.

Progress and accounts

Move my progress to a new phone

Signing in is optional. Without it, progress lives only on your phone. To keep it safe, sign in with Apple, Google or Facebook in Me → Settings → Account, then sign in with the same account on the new phone.

My streak or progress looks wrong

Check that you’re signed in to the same account on every device and that you’re online, then reopen the app so it can sync. If it’s still wrong, email us your Player ID and we’ll look into it.

Delete my account and data

In the app: Me → Settings → Account → Delete account. Without the app, follow the steps on our account deletion page.

Ads and privacy

You can change your ad choices any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices, and turn off personalised ads, analytics and crash reports in Settings → Privacy. Brainik Pro and Remove Ads turn ads off completely. Read the privacy policy for what we collect and why.

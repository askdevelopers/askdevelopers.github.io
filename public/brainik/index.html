Questions

Good to know.

Is Brainik really free?

Yes. All eleven games, every level and the daily garden are free. The free version shows some ads. Brainik Pro is optional and removes them.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play straight away and your progress stays on your phone. If you want a backup or to play on another device, sign in with Apple, Google or Facebook.

Does it work offline?

Yes, the games run on your device. You only need a connection to back up progress, make purchases or see ads.

Is it good for kids?

Brainik is made for adults first, but the Easy ladders are friendly for older kids too. There is no chat and no contact with other players.

Will it make me smarter?

We make no medical claims. Brainik is a set of games that ask you to practise logic, memory, attention and mental maths in a calm way. Most people simply find it a pleasant daily habit.

How do I delete my data?

Delete your account inside the app from Settings, or follow the steps on our account deletion page. Read the privacy policy for what we collect and why.

1. You can play without an account

Every game works without signing in. Your progress is then stored only on your device. Signing in (with Apple, Google or Facebook) is optional and is used to back up your progress and restore it on other devices.

3. Advertising and tracking

The free version of Brainik shows ads served by Google AdMob. On iPhone and iPad, the app asks for permission before your advertising identifier is used to track you across other companies' apps and websites; if you decline, ads are not personalised with it. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, you are asked for consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also turn off personalised ads in Settings → Personalised ads. Subscribers to Brainik Pro and buyers of Remove Ads see no ads.

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We use these service providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring, Cloud Messaging, Remote Config, In-App Messaging, App Check): accounts, cloud save, analytics, crash reports and notifications. Firebase privacy
  • PostHog: product analytics (which screens, games and features are used). We do not send it your name, email or anything you type, and we do not record your screen. PostHog privacy
  • Google AdMob: advertising. How Google uses data from apps that use its services
  • RevenueCat: managing in-app purchases and subscriptions. RevenueCat privacy
  • Apple, Google and Facebook: sign-in, if you choose them.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play: payments. We never see your card details.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

5. How long we keep it

Your account and cloud save are kept until you delete your account. Analytics and crash data are kept for up to 14 months. Purchase records are kept as long as needed for accounting, tax and fraud-prevention obligations.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the app: Me → Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and cloud save. You can also request deletion on the web: Delete your account.
  • Erase progress on this device: Settings → Data → Erase progress on this phone.
  • Analytics, crash reports and personalised ads can be turned off in Settings → Privacy. Turning off Share usage analytics stops both Firebase Analytics and PostHog.
  • Notifications can be turned off in Settings or in your phone's settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the EU or UK GDPR, or US state laws such as the CCPA/CPRA) you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, and to object to processing. Email askdevelopers001@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days.

Grievances (India): if you are unhappy with how we handle your personal data, email askdevelopers001@gmail.com with the subject "Grievance". We will respond within 30 days. If you are still not satisfied, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Cancelling an account does not cancel a subscription. Subscriptions are managed in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

7. Children

Brainik is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Cloud saves are protected by access rules so that only your signed-in account can read or change your data. Purchases are verified with Apple and Google.

9. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States, under appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your data

Delete your RideSaathi account

You can delete your account and its data at any time, in the app or by email.

In the app

  1. Open RideSaathi and tap Me.
  2. Tap Delete my data.
  3. Confirm. You are signed out straight away.

By email

No access to the app? Email us from the address you signed in with, and we will delete the account within 30 days and confirm by reply.

Email a delete request

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in, profile, gender and language
  • Your car details
  • Rides you offered, seat requests and your trip history

Everything above is removed within 30 days. Live trip location is never kept after a trip ends.

What stays for a while

  • Ratings you gave stay in other members' averages, with nothing that links them to you.
  • Safety reports are kept for up to 12 months, so a removed member can't simply start again.
  • Support emails are kept for up to 2 years, unless you ask us to delete them too.
Deleting the app is not enough. Removing RideSaathi from your phone doesn't delete your account. Use the steps above.

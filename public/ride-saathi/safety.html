Safety

Built for people who ride together every day.

You share a car with the same faces most mornings. These tools help everyone feel at ease from the first ride.

  • Women-only rides

    A ride can be marked women-only, so only women can ask for its seats. Women riders can look for just those rides.

  • Ratings both ways

    Drivers and riders rate each other after every trip, so good habits show and problems surface early.

  • See who is in the car

    Before you ride, you see the driver, the car and who sits where.

  • Share your trip

    Send your trip to someone you trust before you set off.

  • SOS

    An SOS button is one tap away during a trip, for when you need help fast.

  • Report and block

    Report a driver or rider for anything that feels wrong, and block them so you are not matched again.

  • No tracking

    RideSaathi doesn't follow you around. Your pickup and drop points are shared only with the people in that car.

  • Cost sharing only

    Shares are capped per km, so nobody drives for profit. Anyone asking for more can be reported.

Good habits

Five small things that help.

  1. Meet at the pickup point shown in the app, somewhere public and well lit.
  2. Check the car: model, colour and number plate should match what the app shows.
  3. Share your trip with someone at home, especially on a first ride.
  4. Pay only the share shown in the app. If anyone asks for more, report it.
  5. Trust your instincts. If something feels off, don't get in, and tell us.
In an emergency, call the police on 112 first. To report a member, use Report in the app or email askdevelopers001@gmail.com.

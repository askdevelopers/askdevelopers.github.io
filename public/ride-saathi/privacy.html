Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

The short version

  • We collect what a shared ride needs: your first name, sign-in, car details if you drive, your routes and pickup points, and ratings.
  • Live location is shared only during a trip, and deleted when the trip ends.
  • We never handle money. Riders pay drivers directly, for example by UPI, outside the app.
  • We never sell your data. You can delete your account at any time.

Who we are

RideSaathi is made by Ask Developers, India. Contact: askdevelopers001@gmail.com. This policy covers the RideSaathi app on Android and iOS and this website.

Payments

RideSaathi never handles money. The share for a ride is paid by the rider straight to the driver, for example by UPI, outside the app. We never see or store card, bank or UPI details.

Permissions

  • Places you choose: the pickup and drop points you pick or search for, to match you with cars on that route. Place searches are sent to an OpenStreetMap search server without your name. We don't track or keep a history of where you go.
  • App Tracking Transparency (iPhone): asked once for personalised ads. Saying no keeps the app fully working.

Who can use RideSaathi

RideSaathi is for adults aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

How long we keep it

  • Live trip location: deleted when the trip ends.
  • Your account, car, rides and trip history: while your account is open. Deleted within 30 days of deleting your account.
  • Reports about safety: up to 12 months after the account is deleted, so a removed member can't simply start again.
  • Analytics: up to 14 months. Support emails: up to 2 years, or sooner if you ask.

Your rights

You can see and change your profile in the app and delete your account at any time. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act and similar laws (such as the GDPR), you can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, or to withdraw consent. Email askdevelopers001@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days. This address is also our contact for privacy complaints.

Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS) and is stored on Google's Firebase servers behind access rules, so members can read only what their rides need.

Changes

If RideSaathi starts collecting something new, this page, the Google Play Data safety form and the App Store privacy label change together, with the date above. Big changes are also announced in the app.

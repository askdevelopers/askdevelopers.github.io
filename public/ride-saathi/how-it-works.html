How it works

Your commute, drawn as a metro line.

Every shared ride is one car on its own route, with stops where riders get on and off. Here is the line, stop by stop.

  1. 01

    Offer a seat

    Driver

    Add your car once, then the route you drive to work and the time you leave. Your empty seats become places that riders along the way can join.

  2. 02

    Find a car going your way

    Rider

    Search with your pickup, drop and time. RideSaathi shows cars whose route passes within a short walk of both, with your drop after your pickup.

    Several riders can join one car at different points, up to the seats on offer.

  3. 03

    See who sits where

    Both

    The car seen from above: who drives, who sits in front, who joins from where, and which seat is still free.

  4. 04

    Follow the line

    Both

    Your trip as a timed line: where the car starts, where each rider gets on and off, and when it reaches your stop.

  5. 05

    Split the fuel

    Both

    Each rider's share is the kilometres they ride times the driver's per-km rate. You settle it directly with the driver, for example by UPI. RideSaathi never handles the money and takes no commission.

  6. 06

    Rate each other

    Both

    After the trip, the driver rates the riders and the riders rate the driver. Good ratings build trust for the next morning.

Two roles, one app

Drive some days, ride on others.

Anyone can do both. The role you pick first only decides your home screen.

When you drive

  • Offer the empty seats on your daily route
  • Choose your per-km share inside the cap
  • Mark a ride women-only if you prefer
  • See who sits where before you set off
  • Rate your riders after the trip

When you ride

  • Find a car whose route passes near you
  • See your seat and who else is in the car
  • Pay only for the kilometres you share
  • Share your trip, with SOS one tap away
  • Rate your driver after the trip
The fair-share rule

Cost sharing, not fares.

A private car may share its running costs, not carry people for profit. So the rate per km is capped.

Driver's rate per km
₹4 suggested
₹0₹2₹4₹6₹8₹10

The band a driver can choose: ₹2 to ₹8 per km

Anita rides 7 km × ₹4 = ₹28

  • No commissionRideSaathi takes nothing from the share.
  • No profitThe cap keeps the share to running costs, which is what private cars may do.
  • Paid straight to the driverSettle up directly, for example by UPI. The app never holds your money.

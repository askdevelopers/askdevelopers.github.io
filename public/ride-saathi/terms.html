The short version

  • RideSaathi helps colleagues and neighbours share a commute and its running costs. It is not a taxi service.
  • Drivers are responsible for a valid licence, insurance and a safe car.
  • No profit and no commercial use of private cars. The share is capped per km.
  • Be kind and be on time. We can remove anyone who puts others at risk.

1. What RideSaathi is

RideSaathi is an app made by Ask Developers, India, that lets people who make the same daily trip share a car and its running costs. A driver offers empty seats on a trip they are making anyway; riders along the route join and share the fuel and running costs for the kilometres they ride.

RideSaathi is not a taxi, cab, ride-hailing or transport service. We don't provide rides, employ or hire drivers, or own cars. Each ride is an arrangement between the members who share it.

2. Who can use it

  • You must be 18 or older and give true details about yourself and, if you drive, your car.
  • One account per person. Keep your sign-in to yourself; you are responsible for what happens in your account.

3. Cost sharing, not profit

  • The driver sets a per-km share inside the band the app allows (currently ₹2 to ₹8 per km). A rider's share is the kilometres they ride times that rate.
  • The share covers running costs only. Drivers must not ask for more than the share shown in the app, charge fixed fares, or make a profit.
  • No commercial use of private cars. Don't use RideSaathi to carry people for hire or reward, to run a taxi or transport business, or to drive trips you would not make anyway.
  • RideSaathi takes no commission on shares.

4. If you drive

You alone are responsible for:

  • holding a valid driving licence for the car you drive;
  • a valid registration, valid insurance (including any cover required for passengers) and a valid pollution certificate;
  • keeping the car safe and roadworthy, and following traffic laws;
  • never driving tired or under the influence, and not using your phone while driving.

5. If you ride

  • Be at the pickup point on time, wear a seat belt and respect the driver, the other riders and the car.
  • Pay the share shown in the app, directly to the driver.

6. Payments

Shares are paid directly between members, for example by UPI. RideSaathi does not collect, hold, refund or guarantee payments, and is not part of any payment between members.

7. Respect and safety

  • No harassment, threats, discrimination or unwanted contact, in a car or in the app.
  • Women-only rides are for women only. Don't misuse them.
  • Ratings must be honest. Reports must be true.
  • We may warn, suspend or remove anyone who breaks these terms or puts others at risk, and we may share information with the police where the law requires it.

8. Ads

RideSaathi is free to use and shows ads. Ads are shown only after you have answered the consent form, and never during a trip. If we offer optional paid features later, their price and terms are shown in the app before you buy.

9. Our responsibility

We work to keep RideSaathi running and safe, but the app is provided "as is". Rides are arranged and taken between members at their own judgement. To the extent the law allows, Ask Developers is not liable for the conduct of members, for accidents, loss or damage during a ride, or for payments between members. Nothing here limits rights you have under Indian consumer law.

10. Ending your account

You can stop using RideSaathi and delete your account at any time. We may close accounts that break these terms.

Your data

Delete your LeafPulse data

LeafPulse has no accounts and no cloud copy of your plants. Everything you add lives on your phone, so deleting it is in your hands and takes a moment.

In the app

  1. Open LeafPulse and tap More.
  2. Scroll to Erase all data and confirm.

Plants, schedules, care history, journal entries, photos, the on-device usage log and your settings are removed straight away. The app starts fresh.

Or delete the app

Removing LeafPulse from your phone removes all of its data too, including photos the app saved in its own folder. Photos you took with the phone's camera app stay in your photo library, as usual.

What we hold about you

  • Nothing that identifies you. There is no account, email or name in LeafPulse.
  • Usage statistics (if you left them on) are anonymous: which screens and features were used, never plant names, notes or photos. They expire after 12 months. Turn them off any time in More.
  • Purchase records for Premium are kept by Apple, Google and our subscription provider as the law requires. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings first (how).
  • Support emails are kept for up to 2 years. Ask us to delete them and we will.

Ask us to delete support emails

Want a copy first? Premium's vacation sitter sheet and the journal share options let you export your plants and notes as text before you erase them.

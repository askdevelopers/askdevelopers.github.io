Billing help

Common questions

I'm not getting reminders

Check that notifications are allowed for LeafPulse in your phone's Settings and that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't hiding them. In the app, More → Reminders sets the hour. On Android, turn off battery optimisation for LeafPulse so the daily reminder isn't dropped.

My plant isn't in the species list

Choose "Other" when adding it and set the watering, feeding, misting and rotating intervals yourself. Every interval can be changed later on the plant's profile.

I marked a job done by mistake

Tap the check again on the same day and the job goes back to its previous date. Nothing is lost.

Where are my plants on my new phone?

LeafPulse keeps everything on the phone and has no accounts, so a phone-to-phone transfer or backup restore (iCloud, Google) brings your plants along. Otherwise they stay on the old phone.

Is Premium shared between my iPhone and Android phone?

No. Apple and Google subscriptions are separate, and there is no account to link them.

A plant didn't make it

We're sorry. On the plant's profile, choose "No longer with me". Its reminders stop and its journal stays, in the quiet Remembered list under More.

Billing

  • Cancel on iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → LeafPulse → Cancel Subscription.
  • Cancel on Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → LeafPulse → Cancel.
  • Refunds: request them from Apple or Google Play. We can't issue refunds ourselves.
  • Restore Premium after reinstalling: More → LeafPulse Premium → Restore purchase, signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account.

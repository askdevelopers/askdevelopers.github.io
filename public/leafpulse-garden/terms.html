1. What LeafPulse is

LeafPulse is a houseplant-care companion: care schedules and reminders, plant profiles, a journal, care guides and, with Premium, extra tools. Care intervals, light needs, pet-safety badges, the leaf doctor, the light meter, plant identification and weather-based adjustments are general guidance built from typical indoor conditions and public references. Plants, homes and seasons differ: check the soil and the plant, and use your own judgement.

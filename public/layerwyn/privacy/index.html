Privacy policy

Layerwyn is a software engineering company headquartered in North York, ON, Canada. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and when you contact us, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We follow Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

  1. What we collect

    • When you send the project inquiry form or email us: your name, your work email, your company if you add it, and what you tell us about your project, such as what you need, a budget range and timing.
    • When you visit: like every website, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical data, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the site and keep it secure. We do not use this data to identify you.
    • We do not use tracking cookies, advertising pixels or analytics tools that follow you. If you switch the site to the light theme, that choice is saved in your own browser only and is never sent to us.

  2. How we use it

    • To reply to your inquiry, plan a first call and prepare a scope and estimate.
    • To deliver and manage your project if we work together.
    • To keep business records and meet our legal and tax obligations.

    We never sell, rent or trade personal information. We will not add you to a newsletter or send you marketing emails unless you clearly ask for them, as Canada’s Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL) requires.

  3. Your consent

    By sending us the form or an email, you agree that we may use your information for the purposes above. You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to us. We will then stop using your information, unless the law requires us to keep it.

  4. Who we share it with

    Only with the people and services we need to answer you and do the work:

    • Ask Developers, our engineering studio in India, which is part of our delivery team.
    • The providers that run our email and host this website.
    • Public authorities, only when the law requires it.

    Everyone who handles your information for us must protect it and use it only for our purposes.

  5. Information stored outside Canada

    Our engineering studio is in India, and some of our service providers store data in the United States and other countries. Your information may therefore be stored or processed outside Canada, where authorities may be able to access it under local law. We protect it with the same care wherever it is.

  6. How long we keep it

    If an inquiry does not lead to a project, we delete it within 24 months of our last contact. Client and project records are kept for as long as our agreement and Canadian law require (for example, six years for tax records), and then deleted.

  7. How we protect it

    Only the people who need your information can see it, and we protect our accounts and devices with strong security measures. This site is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). If a breach ever creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we will tell you and report it to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, as the law requires.

  8. Your rights

    You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. Email us and we will reply within 30 days, free of charge. If you live outside Canada, for example in the European Union, the United Kingdom or California, we will also honour the rights your local privacy law gives you.

  9. Children

    This website is for businesses. It is not meant for children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

  10. Questions and complaints

    Our Privacy Officer, Jay Patel, is responsible for how we handle personal information. Write to askdevelopers001@gmail.com. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

  11. Changes to this policy

    If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new date. If a change affects how we use information you already gave us, we will ask for your consent again.

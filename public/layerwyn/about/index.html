About

A Canadian software company, built to ship.

Layerwyn designs, builds and runs software for companies of every size.

We started Layerwyn for one reason: companies should not need five agencies to ship one product. Design, apps, AI, cloud, security and support belong in one team that owns the result.

Our engineers have shipped store apps, web platforms, CRMs and e-commerce systems that are live today. Now we bring that experience to Canadian businesses.

Co-founder & CEO

North York, ON, Canada

An engineer first. Now the person you talk to.

Jay comes from .NET engineering: C#, ASP.NET Core and SQL Server, building the business systems and APIs that companies run on every day.

Over time his work moved into the cloud and security. He designs and runs systems on Azure, with AWS and Google Cloud when a project needs them, and treats identity, access and data protection as part of the build, not something added at the end.

At Layerwyn he leads the company from our North York headquarters. He runs the first call, the scope and the estimate, and the check-ins after that. When you ask a question, the answer comes from someone who has built this kind of system himself.

SB

Sumit Bhargav

Co-founder & CTO

India

15+ years and 50+ apps that people use every day.

Sumit started as a software engineer and has kept building ever since. Over more than 15 years he has worked on 50+ apps and platforms: native iOS and Android apps, Flutter apps, websites and web platforms, and the Python and Java services behind them.

Most of that work was for clients in Europe and the United States: dating apps, banking, e-commerce, healthcare and, more recently, agentic AI. One of the apps he worked on was recognized by Apple for its design.

Before Layerwyn he was tech lead and principal engineer at YORO Health, an elder-care startup. He made the architecture decisions the platform is built on and led the team that shipped it on the web, iOS and Android.

He also runs Ask Developers, the studio that builds Layerwyn projects and its own apps. His recent work brings AI and machine learning into real products, with security built in from the first line of code.

At Layerwyn he owns how your product is built: the architecture, the code reviews and every release that reaches your users.

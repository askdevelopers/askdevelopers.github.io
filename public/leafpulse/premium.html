How each one works

Tap a tile to see how it works.

The app has the same explainer on every Premium feature, so you know what you get before you decide.

Unlimited plantsHow it works

Basic looks after up to five plants. Premium removes the limit, so every pot on every windowsill gets its own profile and reminders.

Unlimited Leaf doctorHow it works

Basic includes one Leaf doctor check a day. With Premium you can check every leaf that worries you, as often as you like. It still works offline.

Light meterHow it works

Hold your phone where the plant sits. LeafPulse reads the light through the camera and tells you whether the spot is low, medium, bright or direct sun, and which plants will be happy there.

Identify by photoHow it works

Take or choose a photo of the plant and tap Identify. The photo is sent to Pl@ntNet, which suggests the likely species; pick one and its care defaults are filled in for you.

Weather-smart wateringHow it works

With location allowed, LeafPulse checks the forecast from MET Norway. A heat wave brings watering forward, a cold snap pushes it back, and the job tells you why.

Vacation modeHow it works

Enter your trip dates. LeafPulse plans care around them, can pause reminders while you are away, and makes a one-page sitter sheet you can share or print.

The whole growth storyHow it works

Basic shows the last three heights on each growth chart. Premium keeps every height you have logged, so you can see the whole story.

No adsHow it works

Premium removes ads from the app completely.

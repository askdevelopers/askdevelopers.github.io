Website garden

The garden on our home page (the meadow, tree, flowers, butterflies, birds and the vine) is painted live in the browser by our own code. It uses no photos or stock art.

Website sounds

  • Garden birdsong: Birds Waking #3 (BigSoundBank #0999, spring countryside at dawn), CC0. 40 s loop, softened.
  • Watering: Watering tomatoes (BigSoundBank #0818), CC0. 3.5 s cut, softened.
  • Spray: Short spray (BigSoundBank #0044), CC0. One spray, softened.
  • Robin's song on the vine: one 1.6 s phrase from the same Birds Waking #3 recording, CC0, softened.

Data and services

  • Pet-safety badges follow the ASPCA's list of plants toxic and non-toxic to cats and dogs. Informational only.
  • Weather-smart watering (Premium) uses forecasts from the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway), under the Norwegian Licence for Open Government Data (NLOD) and CC BY 4.0.
  • Plant identification (Premium) is powered by Pl@ntNet.
  • Nearby nurseries use place data from Geoapify and OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
  • Type: Manrope by Mikhail Sharanda, SIL Open Font License.

How it grows

Plant it. Water it. Watch it grow.

LeafPulse does the remembering, so you can enjoy the green.

  1. 01Plant

    Add a plant in seconds

    Pick the species and the watering, light and feeding rhythm fills itself in.

  2. 02Water

    Reminded on the right day

    One gentle reminder at the hour you choose, and the week ahead at a glance.

  3. 03Light

    Find the right spot

    The light meter reads a corner through your camera and says which plants will like it.

  4. 04Grow

    Learn as it grows

    Short guides, a leaf doctor for yellow leaves, and a journal for every new leaf.

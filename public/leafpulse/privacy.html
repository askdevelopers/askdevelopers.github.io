Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

The short version

  • LeafPulse has no accounts. Your plants, care schedules, photos, journal and settings live on your phone.
  • Nothing leaves your phone unless you use a feature that needs the internet (listed below), and each one sends the minimum it needs.
  • We never see your plant names, notes or photos. We never sell data.
  • You can turn usage statistics off in More, and erase everything with More → Erase all data or by deleting the app.

Who we are

LeafPulse is made by Ask Developers, India. Contact: askdevelopers001@gmail.com. This policy applies to the LeafPulse app on iOS and Android and to this website.

What stays on your phone

Everything you put into LeafPulse is stored only on your device: plant names, species, rooms, care schedules and care history, journal entries, heights, photos, reminder settings and your preferences. Reminders are local notifications scheduled by the app itself. Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no cloud backup in this version, so a phone backup (iCloud or Google) is the only copy.

Ads in the website mini game

The Pest Patrol game on our home page may show a short ad from Google (AdSense) between rounds, and you can choose to watch one to save a lost garden. Google may use cookies or similar technology to show and measure these ads and, where the law allows and you agree, to personalise them. Visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent first through Google's consent message, and can change their choice at any time. Your game scores are kept only in your own browser. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Permissions

  • Notifications: for care reminders. Optional.
  • Camera and photos: for plant photos, the light meter and identification. Photos stay on the phone except the one you send for identification.
  • Location (while using): for weather-smart watering and nearby nurseries only. Never in the background, never stored as history.
  • App Tracking Transparency (iPhone, Basic plan): asked once for personalised ads. Refusing it keeps the app fully working with non-personalised ads.

Children

LeafPulse is for adults and is not directed at children under 13 (or the age of consent where you live). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Retention

On-device data stays until you erase it. Usage statistics are kept for 12 months. Purchase records are kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat for as long as tax and accounting law requires. Support emails are kept for up to 2 years unless you ask us to delete them.

Your rights

You can see and erase all your data in the app at any time. Where privacy law gives you rights of access, correction, deletion or objection (GDPR, CCPA, India's DPDP Act and others), email us and we will help within 30 days. Because there are no accounts, the only data we could hold about you is in support emails and in anonymous usage statistics.

Security

Data in transit to the services above is encrypted (HTTPS). On-device data is protected by your phone's own encryption and lock.

Changes

If LeafPulse starts sending something new, this page, the App Store privacy label and the Google Play Data safety form change together, with the date above. Material changes are also announced in the app.
